# Synthetic EPC Contract - Change Order Risk

This is a synthetic fixture for testing. It does not describe a real project.

## Change instruction

The owner may adjust project scope, technical requirements, construction sequence, or delivery schedule according to project needs. The contractor shall execute first, and the parties shall negotiate price and schedule adjustment later.

## Risk pattern

The contractor must perform changes before price and schedule impact are confirmed.

## Expected review focus

- Change order approval process.
- Price and schedule adjustment method.
- Written confirmation before execution.
- Evidence retention for claims.
