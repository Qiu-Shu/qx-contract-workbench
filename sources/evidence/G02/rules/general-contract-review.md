# General Contract Review Rules

These rules are public review prompts, not legal advice.

## Subject and authority

- Check whether party names are complete and consistent.
- Check whether the signer or agent authority is stated or should be confirmed.
- Flag inconsistent party names as `to_confirm` or higher depending on impact.

## Subject matter and scope

- Check whether the deliverables, services, goods, quantities, specifications, and acceptance standards are concrete enough to perform.
- Flag references to missing attachments or undefined schedules.

## Price and payment

- Check payment amount, currency, tax treatment, invoice requirements, payment milestones, and late-payment responsibility.
- Flag vague triggers such as "after acceptance" when acceptance standards or timing are undefined.

## Delivery and acceptance

- Check delivery time, location, risk transfer, acceptance procedure, objection period, and evidence of delivery.
- Flag missing acceptance criteria that may delay payment or performance confirmation.

## Breach and liability

- Check whether liability is reciprocal and proportionate.
- Flag unlimited liability, one-sided penalties, missing late-payment responsibility, and broad indirect-loss exposure.

## Termination

- Check termination events, cure periods, notice requirements, and settlement after termination.
- Flag unilateral termination rights that are too broad or unclear.

## Confidentiality and intellectual property

- Check confidentiality scope, permitted disclosure, survival period, ownership of background and foreground IP, and use restrictions.
- Flag missing confidentiality obligations when technical materials, customer data, or trade secrets are involved.

## Dispute resolution

- Check governing law, court jurisdiction, arbitration institution, dispute escalation, and enforceability of the forum clause.
- Flag ambiguous forum wording or inconsistent court/arbitration choices.
