# Sales Contract Review Rules

Apply these rules to sales, procurement, supply, product delivery, and equipment purchase contracts.

## Product description

- Confirm product name, model, quantity, unit, technical specifications, quality standards, and accessory scope.
- Flag "see attachment" references when the attachment is missing or not versioned.

## Payment safety

- Review whether payment milestones match delivery, acceptance, and invoice obligations.
- Flag back-to-back payment clauses, vague acceptance-triggered payments, and missing late-payment responsibility.

## Delivery risk

- Review delivery method, logistics responsibility, insurance, risk transfer, and proof of receipt.
- Flag delivery locations described only as "designated place" without objective confirmation process.

## Acceptance and quality objection

- Review acceptance standards, acceptance timeline, inspection method, and objection procedure.
- Flag deemed acceptance clauses that are too short or lack evidence requirements.

## Warranty and maintenance

- Review warranty period, start date, service response, excluded causes, replacement obligations, and cost allocation.
- Flag warranty periods or performance guarantees that are unclear or commercially unusual.

## Breach balance

- Review seller and buyer breach responsibilities together.
- Flag high seller penalties without matching buyer late-payment obligations.

## Suggested reviewer output

For each finding, state:

- Contract basis.
- Risk explanation.
- Suggested negotiation or revision direction.
- Whether business, finance, technical, or legal confirmation is needed.
