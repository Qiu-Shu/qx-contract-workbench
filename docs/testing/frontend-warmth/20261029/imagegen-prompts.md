## 首页设计稿

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high fidelity desktop application UI design, a single full screen, target 1440x900 landscape. Create a polished real-world Chinese lawyer's daily workbench homepage named “群兴 法律工作台”. This is a new visual design for an existing production app, not a marketing landing page, not a device mockup. The key idea is “法理之外的温情”: professional clarity and dignity with quiet human warmth, a place to think calmly about complicated human situations. The previous design was too cold, blue, boxy and bureaucratic. Make a distinctive, exquisitely composed product interface with realistic generous spacing, meticulous optical alignment, soft layered paper surfaces, crisp readable Chinese typography, restrained borders and subtle shadows. Light warm-stone sidebar, porcelain main surface, muted periwinkle/slate-indigo primary actions, warm graphite text, a tiny restrained apricot or sage accent. No navy sidebar, no black/acid-green scheme, no terracotta-on-cream template, no gaudy gradient, no oversized pill buttons everywhere. Warmth must come from rhythm, typographic contrast, gentle specific language and thoughtful grouping, not from cartoon mascots.

Layout and real functions: left persistent sidebar about 220px, brand at top “群兴” / “法律工作台”, a clearly labeled “新对话” button with plus icon, a working navigation row “工作区与接待”, “事项记录” followed by two fictional conversation names “借贷咨询｜王某与李某” and “新对话”. Bottom accordion “业务能力”, a quiet logged-in account “界面演示律师”. Main application top bar with small “事项工作台”, honest “本机草稿” status and “阅读字号 标准16”. Main page has a human editorial heading exactly “把事情，一件件理清。” with small supporting copy “先记下眼前的情况，重要的线索可以逐步补齐。” Give the composition a refined signature: a small atmospheric photographic still-life in a narrow upper-right vignette, only a beautifully made blank paper folio, soft natural window light and one small stem in a simple ceramic vase, no visible legal documents or people's private data; this should be an elegant quiet accent, never a big banner. The active work area must remain the hero.

A quiet toolbar contains five real expandable tools “档案草稿”, “接待底稿”, “事实与材料”, “待补与跟进”, “成果与导出”. They are actions, not a one-selected-only tab bar. Below, one dominant white writing surface on the left: tiny label “咨询内容与工作指令”, a large empty multiline textarea with placeholder “写下客户的情况、你的问题，或希望完成的工作…”, bottom action row “添加材料”, secondary “保存草稿”, primary “发送” (visually disabled in this empty state). A plain footer says “尚未输入” and “输入后会保存在本机，已有内容可随时继续整理。” Do not claim actual AI generation or successful save for an empty field. On the right, a compact secondary “工作方式” area contains four refined icon+title+description choices “客户接待 / 理清诉求，准备下一步”, “案件整理 / 梳理事实与材料线索”, “审查合同 / 核对原文，准备修改建议”, “庭审准备 / 从不同视角准备追问”. These choices should be beautifully proportioned, calm and tactile, not four identical floating generic dashboard cards. Include a small collapsed “当前可用范围与说明” below the writing area.

Body text 14-16px, supporting text at least12px, fine line icons, two harmonious type roles at most, optionally a restrained graceful Chinese display serif for only the heading. No fake metrics, charts, calendar, notifications, avatars of real people, invented case counts, random English, unimplemented functions or extra controls. All example data fictional. Show one coherent deployable-looking screen edge to edge, no explanatory labels outside the app, no watermarks. Make this feel like a carefully art-directed premium tool for actual lawyers: intellectually precise, quietly warm, highly usable. Render all given Chinese labels accurately and keep everything comfortably within the full viewport.

## 独立案头静物素材

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single panoramic photographic masthead background asset for a real Chinese legal workbench desktop app. The attached UI design is only the art-direction reference. Generate ONLY the photographic still-life area from its upper-right header, not any of its UI, type, buttons, icons or layout. Output a wide 3:1 landscape image, ideally 1536x512. A quietly warm, sophisticated lawyer's writing desk in gentle natural morning window light: one beautifully bound blank pale paper folio lying closed on warm oak, a slim understated pen, and one small unglazed ivory ceramic vase with a delicate natural green branch at the far right. The left two thirds must remain very light warm-porcelain negative space with only soft natural window shadows, near color #F8F6F3, so real application text can be placed there in code. Objects only in the rightmost third and lower right; plenty of clear breathing room at top and left. Clean contemporary Chinese professional sensibility, humane calm, restrained premium editorial photography, truthful tactile paper/wood/ceramic textures, subtle warm daylight, neither orangey nor sepia. A smooth photographic transition from blank wall to desk without artificial shapes. No people, no faces, no screens, no gavel or scales, no legible paper content, no branding, no typography, no letters, no watermark, no interface frame. The entire output must be just the natural photographic scene.
